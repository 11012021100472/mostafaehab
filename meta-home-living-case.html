From Product Testing to Profitable Scaling

The journey

More than a revenue screenshot.

The work began with testing different home, kitchen, organization, and décor products alongside multiple creative formats. Once the strongest products and messages became clear, the focus shifted to scaling without losing efficiency.

Across six months, the approach moved from discovery to a more deliberate acquisition and retargeting structure, with ongoing creative and performance analysis.

What changed

  • Product testing: tested 20+ products and varied UGC, video, and static creatives.
  • Winner identification: top-performing products reportedly contributed around 70% of revenue.
  • Smarter scaling: structured Conversion and Retargeting activity to balance new customer acquisition and follow-up.
  • Efficiency improvement: reported ROAS improved from 2.8x in month one to 6.6x by month six.
